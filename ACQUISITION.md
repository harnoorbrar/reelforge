# ReelForge — Customer Acquisition Playbook (zero budget)

## The loop (do this daily)
1. **Make 3 Reels with ReelForge** about trending faceless topics
   (passive income, AI tools, productivity, phone tips).
2. **Post** to TikTok + Reels + Shorts. The video IS the product demo.
3. **Pin the landing-page CTA** in bio + first comment.
4. **Reply to comments** with "link in bio — generate yours free."

## Channels
- **TikTok / Reels / Shorts** — primary. Faceless content, daily.
- **Reddit** — r/Entrepreneur, r/PassiveIncome, r/TikTokgrowth (value-first, no spam).
- **X/Twitter** — post Reels + "made with ReelForge" threads.
- **Creator FB groups** — offer 10 free Reels for a testimonial.

## Offer / pricing (freemium)
- **Free**: 3-scene render, watermark. (Costs you $0 on free generators.)
- **Pro $9/mo**: no watermark, 5 scenes, all 4 styles, priority.
- **Agency $29/mo**: bulk + API access.

## Cold outreach script (DM creators)
"Hey — I built a tool that turns any topic into a faceless Reel
(script + voice + visuals) in 2 min. Want 10 free ones for your niche?
Just reply with your niche."

## Upgrade levers (when you have $)
- Add `OPENAI_API_KEY` → smarter scripts.
- Add `FAL_KEY` → sharper images.
- Add a music library → better soundtracks.
- Stripe → paid tier + watermark removal.

## Metrics to watch
Reels posted/day · profile visits · signups · free→paid conversion.
